Assignment 7.2 — Pricing Cards & Centered Login Form

Aim

To create a webpage containing three pricing cards and a centered login form using CSS Flexbox and alignment properties.

Flexbox Properties Used

- "display: flex" — Creates a flexible layout.
- "flex-direction: row" — Arranges the pricing cards horizontally.
- "flex-direction: column" — Arranges card contents and login form fields vertically.
- "justify-content: center" — Centers items along the main axis.
- "align-items: center" — Aligns items along the cross axis.
- "align-items: stretch" — Allows pricing cards to stretch equally in height.
- "flex-wrap: wrap" — Moves items to another line when space is limited.
- "gap" — Adds spacing between cards.

Features

- Three pricing cards: Basic, Pro, and Premium.
- Price, features list, and button for each plan.
- Centered login form with email and password fields.
- Flexbox-based layout and alignment.
- Hover effects on buttons.
- Responsive design for smaller screens.

Files Included

- "index.html" — HTML structure for pricing cards and login form.
- "style.css" — Flexbox styling, alignment, and responsive design.
